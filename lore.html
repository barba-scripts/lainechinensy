The Gospel of the Heretic Lich

Truth be told, there are those brazen sheep that stray away from the Choir's teachings. Shall we uncover the unsung truth of a madman? They say that the Falzchen Librarium holds the key to discover truth beyond eternity. This is the path of folly, but their curiosity may yet be rewarded. Though the Priestess does not endorse their teachings, you may proceed to the library of the heretic Scribe-Scryer Zaleri, at your own peril...
