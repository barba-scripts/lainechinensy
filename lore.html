What is the Falzchen?

“The Falzchen” is the title of my lore-building project. It is a post-apocalyptic sci-fi setting following the Remnants, the survivors of humanity who lived through the Devouring of Heaven: an apocalyptic event wherein the great Voidbeast Kuromega devoured the stars of the universe.

What is the Falzchen Librarium?

In the in-universe lore, the Falzchen is the name of a library-ship floating in space, serving as a repository of lost knowledge. It is operated by the mysterious Father Zaleri, an archivist-scientist, and their crew of “lainedolls”—artificial, biomechanical beings that serve as its librarians.
