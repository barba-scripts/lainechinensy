An introduction

“Listen to my song, dear Voidlings.”

Laine Chinensy

Dearest Voidlings, I am Laine Chinensy, the Void Priestess.

I'm a guitarist and composer, creating original music primarily in the alternative rock and post-rock genres. I'm also an amateur artist, drawing characters from "The Falzchen", which is my original story concept and universe. From time to time, I share my lore and world-building notes about my world as well.

I have been composing music for more than 10 years. I started as a dark ambient composer, making ambient soundscapes with synthesizers. Later, I started composing amateur orchestral music, making musical scores for video games that don't exist. At some point, I took a long hiatus after burnout, but my passion for music was rekindled when I picked up the guitar.
