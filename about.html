Laine Chinensy

Dearest Voidlings, I am Laine Chinensy, the Void Priestess.

Apart from my duties as a Priestess, I live an alternate life as a guitarist and composer, mostly known for my alternative and post-rock style.

I've been composing music for more than ten years. I started out making dark ambient soundscapes with synthesizers, then ventured into amateur orchestral music: scores for video games that don't exist.

After a long hiatus brought on by creative burnout, my passion for music was rekindled when I picked up the guitar. As fate would have it, watching one too many girls' band anime had finally caught up with me. Now, you can find me on the VoidTube, where I upload my Song-sermons.

I'm also an amateur visual artist (anime, *gasp*), often drawing characters from The Falzchen, my original story concept and universe. You can find my art on Cara and Instagram. I love writing nerdy lore and building worlds. Check out the Falzchen Librarium for more!
