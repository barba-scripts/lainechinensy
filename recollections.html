What are Recollections?

In the world of The Falzchen, the universe I'm writing, Recollections are small slice-of-life memory logs or diegetic, in-universe records that reveal fragments of the story.

Do I need to read them in order?

They aren't written chronologically or from a single point of view. Think of them as ambient stories rather than a narrative you have to follow in sequence.
